# Homepage

- **Scope and mode:** Single-page portfolio in Experience mode. The work leads; navigation and decoration recede.
- **Audience and job:** International recruiters and engineering teams should identify Gabriel as a backend-focused product engineer, verify his experience through real projects, and reach a project or contact link quickly.
- **Proof and sequence:** Hero system map → current role → experience → HoyTurnos, PixoAI, Paytor, CEEI → engineering problems → toolbox → education and personal principles → direct contact.
- **Direction:** “Systems log” — an editorial engineering notebook organized with the connected topology and trace rhythm of an observability viewer. Warm off-white paper in daylight, near-black ink, sparse deep teal signal color, fine rules, compact metadata, and authored CSS/SVG system diagrams.
- **Memorable moment:** The first viewport visibly routes PRODUCTS through Gabriel’s BACKEND node toward APIs and DATA, with one slow signal traveling the path. Project rows inherit this topology without becoming generic cards.
- **Component grammar:** Mostly square or lightly softened corners; 1px rules; no decorative shadows; restrained surfaces; large humanist grotesk headlines paired with compact mono only for metadata and system labels.
- **Approved composition:** Choice delegated through the confirmed `spec.md` brief. Asymmetric 7/5 desktop hero, thin current-status register, vertical experience trace, and four large editorial project chapters with varied visual density.
- **Media inventory:** System diagrams use semantic HTML and authored SVG/CSS. HoyTurnos, PixoAI, Paytor, and CEEI use real public project imagery where available. Text, navigation, CTAs, and diagrams remain semantic code.
- **Responsive behavior:** Two-column hero and horizontal project chapters on desktop; a single reading column on tablet/mobile; diagrams simplify rather than scroll; controls remain at least 44px where touch-operated.
- **Constraints:** English copy, light and dark themes, reduced motion, no fabricated evidence, no confidential employer details, no technology-logo wall, and no portfolio-template project cards.
