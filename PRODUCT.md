## Users

Engineering leaders, recruiters, and product teams evaluating Gabriel Monutti for international or remote backend, full-stack, and product-oriented engineering roles.

## Product Purpose

Present Gabriel as a backend-focused engineer with 3+ years of professional experience who builds real business applications, APIs, integrations, and digital products. A visitor should understand his focus, professional credibility, and strongest work within the first minute, then be able to visit a project or contact him directly.

## Positioning

The portfolio demonstrates how Gabriel connects products, external APIs, data, and real-world business operations. It emphasizes shipped systems and engineering judgment rather than technology badges, tutorial projects, or résumé-style claims.

## Operating Context

This is a public, English-language, single-page portfolio deployed on Vercel. Most visitors will arrive from a job application, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a direct introduction and will scan it quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with evidence of real work, not claims about passion or potential.
2. Make backend and integration expertise understandable without requiring specialist knowledge.
3. Let each project show a different dimension: product ownership, mobile development, payments infrastructure, and client delivery.
4. Keep the experience fast to scan, rewarding to explore, and easy to maintain.
5. Prefer precise, simple communication over buzzwords and spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: sufficient contrast, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable without horizontal scrolling from mobile through desktop.
